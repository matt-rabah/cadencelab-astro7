# Fit Check

The Fit Check is Cadence Lab's engagement qualification process. It determines
whether an operating problem is ready for structured diagnostic investigation.

## When to use

- You have a customer experience problem that affects retention, revenue, service
  quality, or team capacity.
- The problem spans functions, systems, or decision layers and cannot be solved
  by one team in isolation.
- Leadership can sponsor cross-functional decisions and assign accountable owners.
- The organization is willing to change workflows instead of placing new
  technology on top of a broken process.

## How it works

1. Share the operating problem, the teams and systems involved, and the outcome
   that needs to change.
2. Cadence Lab assesses fit directly and responds with a structured evaluation.
3. If the problem fits, the engagement moves to a scoped diagnostic.

## Entry point

Complete the Fit Check form at [cadencelab.co/fit-check/](https://cadencelab.co/fit-check/).
