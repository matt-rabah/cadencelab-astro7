# CX Systems Diagnostic

Cadence Lab's CX Systems Diagnostic traces recurring customer friction through
workflows, ownership, CRM signals, service rules, and cross-functional handoffs.

## When to use

- Onboarding completion rates are declining and the cause spans multiple teams.
- Customer friction is recurring but no single system or team owns the problem.
- CRM data, automation rules, and reporting do not reflect how work is actually
  performed.
- Service handoffs between functions (sales, onboarding, support, success) are
  unreliable.

## What the diagnostic produces

1. A shared evidence base documenting the operating environment.
2. A map of workflows, ownership, decision rights, and handoff points.
3. Specific operating recommendations tied to evidence rather than assumptions.
4. A practical sequence for implementing change.

## Entry point

Start a diagnostic engagement by completing a
[Fit Check](https://cadencelab.co/fit-check/) to determine whether the problem
is ready for structured investigation.
